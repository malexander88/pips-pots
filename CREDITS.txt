Changes: each track was cut to a seamless loop (the Kevin MacLeod tracks with a
1.5 s equal-power crossfade at a bar-aligned point), loudness-matched and
re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis. "Daily Beetle" was cut to a 40-bar loop
(57.05 s to 153.64 s of the original) with the same 1.5 s crossfade.
"dailybeetle-intro" is the first 12 s of that same loop, so the menu music can
start at once while the full loop loads.
"Träumerei" was trimmed of its leading and trailing silence (0.45 s to 197.6 s of the
recording), looped with a 1.5 s equal-power crossfade (it is not a seamless loop),
loudness-matched to -20 LUFS and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis.

"Prelude in C" was trimmed of its leading silence (1.39 s to 155.1 s of the
recording, ending as the last chord rings), looped by overlapping the final chord's
2.2 s ring-out with the opening, loudness-matched to -20 LUFS and re-encoded as Ogg
Vorbis. "prelude-intro" is the first 12 s of that same loop (as for every track).

Piano: sounds made in the app.
